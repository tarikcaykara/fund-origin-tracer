Methodology Starting from the seed transaction, the tracer lists earlier incoming transfers of each address and scores every candidate on four factors: same asset, amount proximity, time proximity and a dust penalty. The highest-scoring candidates are followed, up to 3 branches per address. Every dropped candidate is recorded with its reason (exceeds max branches, same tx, unpriced, self transfer). A branch stops at a labelled entity (trace through entities is off), at max depth 6, when no candidate remains, on a cycle, or on a fetch error. Each stop reason is kept on the edge and counted in the unresolved exposure.
9. Limitations Tracing is heuristic: funds are fungible and a followed transfer is the best-scoring candidate, not proof that specific coins moved. Labels cover only the bundled set of 2026-10-20 and no local labels. Tokens without a USD price were not followed.
